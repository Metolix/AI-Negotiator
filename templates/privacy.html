Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect input messages and negotiation text sent through the simulation input area. We do not require account registration or collect personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Processing via Third Party AI (Groq Inc.)

Your inputs are transmitted to third party AI providers (such as Groq Inc.) to generate real time character responses. Data sent to these APIs is processed according to Groq's Privacy Policy. Please do not submit real world sensitive personal information.

3. Session Data & Local Storage

The Application uses temporary session variables and in memory game state to maintain your active scenario session. This data is reset upon starting a new scenario or clearing your browser cookies.

4. Analytics & Security Logs

Standard web server logs (such as IP addresses and browser headers) may be automatically logged for security monitoring, server health evaluation, and protection against rate limit abuse.

5. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this privacy policy or project security, visit the official developer repository on GitHub.
